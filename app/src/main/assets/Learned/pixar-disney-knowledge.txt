Pixar and Disney

Pixar

Pixar was established as an independent company in 1986 after Steve Jobs purchased Lucasfilm's Computer Division.

Pixar originally worked with computer graphics, animation technology, and other forms of digital production.

Pixar created Luxo Jr., one of its early famous animated short films.

Pixar and Disney began working together before Pixar became a major feature-film studio.

Toy Story was released in 1995 and was the world's first fully computer-animated feature film.

Toy Story became a major success and helped establish Pixar as an important animation studio.

Pixar later produced many successful animated films, including A Bug's Life, Toy Story 2, Monsters, Inc., Finding Nemo, The Incredibles, Cars, Ratatouille, WALL-E, Up, and others.

Disney and Pixar worked together for many years before Disney acquired Pixar.

Disney

The Walt Disney Company is an entertainment company known for animation, films, television, theme parks, and other forms of entertainment.

Disney was founded by Walt Disney and Roy O. Disney.

Disney became one of the most influential companies in the history of animation and entertainment.

Disney and Pixar

Disney and Pixar first worked together on computer animation technology and later established a major film partnership.

Disney announced an agreement to acquire Pixar in 2006.

Disney completed the acquisition of Pixar on May 5, 2006.

Pixar continued operating as a distinct animation studio after becoming part of Disney.

The partnership between Disney and Pixar combined Disney's long history in animation and entertainment with Pixar's computer animation technology and storytelling.

Toy Story became one of the most recognizable examples of the collaboration between Disney and Pixar.

Pixar continues to create animated films and other projects as part of The Walt Disney Company.

Random Knowledge

Pixar and Disney were already collaborating before Disney acquired Pixar.

Pixar's first feature film, Toy Story, became an important milestone in computer animation.
