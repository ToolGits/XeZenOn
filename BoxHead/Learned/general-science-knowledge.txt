General Science Knowledge

Physics

Matter has mass and occupies space.

Force can change the motion of an object.

Energy can be transferred or transformed from one form to another.

Gravity is an attractive force between objects with mass.

Chemistry

Atoms are the basic units of chemical elements.

Molecules are formed when atoms bond together.

Chemical reactions transform substances into new substances.

Biology

Living organisms are made of cells.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life.

Plants use photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy.

Animals obtain energy by consuming nutrients.

Astronomy

Stars are massive objects that produce energy through nuclear fusion.

Planets orbit stars due to gravity.

The Solar System contains the Sun, planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

Earth Science

Earth has a solid surface, a liquid outer core, and a solid inner core.

Earth's atmosphere is a layer of gases surrounding the planet.

The water cycle continuously moves water through the atmosphere, land, and oceans.
