Historical Knowledge

Ancient History

Ancient civilizations developed complex societies, governments, cultures, and systems of writing.

Ancient Egypt developed along the Nile River.

Ancient Greece influenced philosophy, science, politics, literature, and art.

The Roman civilization became one of the largest and most influential civilizations of the ancient world.

Medieval History

The Middle Ages followed the decline of the Western Roman Empire and lasted for many centuries.

Medieval societies developed different political, economic, cultural, and religious institutions.

The Renaissance

The Renaissance was a major cultural movement that began in Europe and emphasized art, knowledge, science, and classical learning.

Modern History

The Industrial Revolution transformed manufacturing, transportation, cities, and economies.

The 20th century saw major technological, political, social, and scientific changes.

Contemporary History

The development of computers, the internet, space exploration, and modern medicine transformed human society.
